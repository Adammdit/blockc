  <p>
    Based in Dublin, Ireland, BlockC is an alternative rock band made up of 6 talented musicians. Bonded by a blend of ages, life experiences and love of music – we have set out to achieve thought provoking, kick-ass music… 
  </p>
  <p>
    "Heather Road" is the band’s first release. A journey that started at Heather Road Studios, ended in a hands-on recording session. The EP is a combination of styles and ideas as the band looked to understand their own unique sound. 
  </p>
  <p>
    August 2025. BlockC Returns with "Stranger to Myself"
  </p>
  <p>
    A powerful new album that captures the emotional intensity of a world in flux. Written and recorded by the band over 4 years, the album transforms uncertainty into a bold collection of songs that is anything but restrained.
  </p>
  <p>
    Rooted in BlockC’s signature rock sound, the album expands far beyond expectations. Each band member brings their own musical ideas forward to create a sonically rich and unpredictable listening experience. The result is a dynamic, deeply textured Prog-Rock Album that remains uniquely BlockC.
  </p>
  <p>
    Lyrically, the album explores themes of isolation, resilience, and personal rediscovery. While not a concept album in the traditional sense, a clear emotional arc emerges—charting a journey from confinement to catharsis.
  </p>
  <p>
    From Rock ballads to explosive guitar-driven anthems, BlockC’s latest work is both a reflection of the times and a reimagining of what music can become when artistic boundaries dissolve.
  </p>
</div>
